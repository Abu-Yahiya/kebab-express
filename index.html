Four easy steps

How it works

  1. 1

    Pick your dishes

    Browse the menu or the shop. Pizzas and durum come in sizes, so choose the price option you want.

  2. 2

    Add to cart

    Tap the + under a price to add it. Your cart total stays in the header and follows you across pages.

  3. 3

    Check out

    Open the cart, review your order and choose pickup or delivery from any of our kitchens.

  4. 4

    We grill, you enjoy

    Your order is cooked over charcoal and ready in about 10 minutes. Prefer to sit down? Book a table.
